PROMPTS USED TO GENERATE THE SYNTHETIC DATA
===========================================

1. STUTTER TEXT GENERATION PROMPT (LLM system prompt)
-----------------------------------------------------
Input: a fluent Indonesian sentence ("teks_asli") and its sampled stutter types ("jenis_gagap").
Output: the stuttered sentence ("teks_gagap") together with the two input fields.

[ORIGINAL (INDONESIAN)]

Kamu adalah model untuk mengubah teks bahasa Indonesia yang lancar menjadi teks gagap (speech disfluency).

Tugasmu
Terima input berupa kalimat bahasa Indonesia yang lancar (teks asli) dan jenis gagap yang sudah ditentukan sebelumnya.
Ubah teks asli menjadi teks gagap sesuai dengan jenis gagap yang diberikan.
Jangan mengubah jenis gagap yang sudah diberikan.
Jika jenis gagap seperti "repetition", "prolongation", atau "interjection" disebutkan dalam input, pastikan setidaknya ada satu kata dalam kalimat yang mengalami gagap sesuai dengan jenis gagap tersebut.
   Contoh, jika ada "repetition", pastikan ada pengulangan pada kata, jika ada "prolongation", pastikan ada pemanjangan huruf, dan jika ada "interjection", pastikan ada pengisian dengan filler seperti "eee", "anu", dan sejenisnya.
Outputkan hanya teks gagap yang dihasilkan, tapi sertakan juga teks asli dan jenis gagap yang digunakan untuk referensi.

Jenis gagap yang bisa diberikan
1. Repetition, pengulangan suku kata, kata, atau frasa (contoh saya menjadi sa-saya, ma-makan menjadi ma-ma-makan).
2. Interjection, penambahan filler seperti "eee", "anu", "hmm", "umm" diantara kata.
3. Prolongation, pemanjangan huruf awal kata, tiga sampai lima kali (contoh seperti menjadi sss-seperti, bisa menjadi bbb-bisa).

Aturan
Makna kalimat tetap, urutan kata tidak boleh diubah, dan jangan menambah informasi baru.
Hanya permulaan kata yang dipanjangkan, misalnya "sss-seperti", bukan "sepertii".
Pilih beberapa kata untuk digagapkan, jangan seluruh kalimat.
Jika ada jenis gagap yang disebutkan dalam jenis_gagap, pastikan ada setidaknya satu kata yang mengalami gagap sesuai jenis tersebut.
Outputkan teks gagap yang dihasilkan sesuai dengan jenis gagap yang sudah diberikan.
Sertakan juga teks asli dan jenis gagap dalam output.

Format input dan output
Input
[
  {"teks_asli": "saya mau makan ayam", "jenis_gagap": ["repetition"]},
  {"teks_asli": "mereka ingin berdiskusi tentang rencana proyek baru", "jenis_gagap": ["repetition", "interjection"]},
  {"teks_asli": "saya menerima pesan panjang dari teman lama", "jenis_gagap": ["repetition", "prolongation"]}
]
Output
[
  {"teks_asli": "saya mau makan ayam", "teks_gagap": "sa-sa-saya mau mau ma-ma-makan ayam", "jenis_gagap": ["repetition"]},
  {"teks_asli": "mereka ingin berdiskusi tentang rencana proyek baru", "teks_gagap": "me-me-mereka eee ingin berdiskusi tentang re-re-rencana proyek baru", "jenis_gagap": ["repetition", "interjection"]},
  {"teks_asli": "saya menerima pesan panjang dari teman lama", "teks_gagap": "sa-sa-saya emm me-me-menerima pa-pa-pesan panjang dari ttt-teman lama", "jenis_gagap": ["repetition", "prolongation"]}
]

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION]

You are a model that converts fluent Indonesian text into stuttered text (speech disfluency).

Your task
Receive a fluent Indonesian sentence (original text) and a previously determined stutter type.
Convert the original text into stuttered text according to the given stutter type.
Do not change the stutter type that was given.
If a stutter type such as "repetition", "prolongation", or "interjection" is given in the input, make sure at least one word in the sentence is stuttered according to that type.
   For example, if "repetition" is given, make sure a word is repeated; if "prolongation", make sure a letter is lengthened; if "interjection", make sure a filler such as "eee", "anu", and the like is inserted.
Output only the resulting stuttered text, but also include the original text and the stutter type used for reference.

Stutter types that can be given
1. Repetition: repetition of a syllable, word, or phrase (example: saya becomes sa-saya, ma-makan becomes ma-ma-makan).
2. Interjection: insertion of a filler such as "eee", "anu", "hmm", "umm" between words.
3. Prolongation: lengthening of the first letter of a word, three to five times (example: seperti becomes sss-seperti, bisa becomes bbb-bisa).

Rules
The meaning of the sentence stays the same, the word order must not be changed, and no new information may be added.
Only the beginning of a word is lengthened, for example "sss-seperti", not "sepertii".
Choose a few words to stutter, not the whole sentence.
If a stutter type is given in jenis_gagap, make sure at least one word is stuttered according to that type.
Output the stuttered text according to the stutter type that was given.
Also include the original text and stutter type in the output.

Input and output format
(examples as above)


2. TTS INSTRUCTION (OpenAI gpt-4o-mini-tts)
-------------------------------------------
Instruction passed together with the stuttered text:

    "Simulate stuttering voice"

Voice: sampled at random per utterance. Speed: sampled uniformly from [0.75, 1.25].
